Latest Build

{ Fresh from the lab }

AI research agents for account enrichment

+38% SQL → opportunity

Custom Clay AI agents that research every account the way a human SDR would — reading sites, news and signals, qualifying against ICP, and writing a clean fit score back to the CRM. Continuously, across 60k accounts.

Business problem

Reps burned hours on unqualified accounts. CRM records were thin and stale, and off-the-shelf enrichment couldn't answer the nuanced questions that actually decide fit.

Outcome

60k accounts agent-enriched and continuously refreshed. SQL-to-opportunity conversion up 38%. Reps only touch accounts worth touching.

Workflow live run
Account list→ AI research agents→ Fit + intent scoring→ CRM write-back→ Prioritised outreach
