Topic / Revenue Systems

GTM that compounds.

A campaign ends. A system compounds. That's the whole argument. Most GTM teams run on effort — every quarter starts from zero, every result costs the same as the last one. A revenue system is the alternative: infrastructure that keeps producing long after you set it up.

The anatomy of a revenue system

One lead flow. Every source — website, paid, ads, events — lands in the same scored, routed pipeline. Nobody checks five inboxes. The routing engine took median response from 4 hours to 11 minutes because unification, not urgency, is what makes speed possible.

Data that maintains itself. CRM records rot. A system re-enriches them continuously — AI agents keep 60k accounts fresh so reps never work from stale information.

Attribution you can defend. When every touch is stitched to closed revenue in one dbt model, budget arguments end. $1.1M moved to what actually closes.

Self-serve tooling. If the GTM team queues behind ops for every report, the system has a bottleneck. Internal tools remove it.

The test

Turn the team off for a week. Does pipeline still move? In a campaign shop, everything stops. In a systems shop, visitors get identified, leads get routed, accounts get enriched, follow-ups fire. That's the difference — and it's achievable. GTM engineering is how; AI is the accelerant.
